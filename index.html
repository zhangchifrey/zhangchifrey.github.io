张驰 Chi Zhang

Chi Zhang | 同济大学博士后 张驰 | Postdoctoral Researcher, Tongji University

同济大学经济与管理学院 · 管理高等研究院 School of Economics and Management · Advanced Institute of Business, Tongji University

研究方向聚焦于管理学领域的社会议题，核心关注残疾包容与污名化员工群体相关问题。具体而言，致力于探究商业场景中弱势群体的能动性如何有效改善其不利处境。我坚信，严谨的学术研究能够助力构建更美好、更公正的社会。

My research interests broadly situate in social issues in management, with a primary focus on disability inclusion and stigmatized workers. Specifically, I am dedicated to exploring how the agency of vulnerable groups in the business context can successfully ameliorate their disadvantaged situations. I believe that rigorous research can help build a better and more just society.

2025年12月至今，任同济大学经济与管理学院博士后，所属机构为管理高等研究院。2018年9月至2025年12月在同济大学攻读管理学博士（组织行为学），指导教师为梁建教授。2024年3月至10月，于美国密苏里大学杜拉斯克（Trulaske）商学院管理学系访问，指导教师为 John Schaubroeck 与 Ann Peng 教授。

Since December 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Economics and Management, Tongji University, affiliated with the Advanced Institute of Business. I received my Ph.D. in Management (Organizational Behavior) from Tongji University (2018–2025), advised by Prof. Jian Liang (梁建). From March to October 2024, I was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Management Department at the Trulaske College of Business, University of Missouri, advised by Prof. John Schaubroeck and Prof. Ann Peng.
